# Heparin Protocol Calculations: A Step-by-Step Guide

Heparin calculations are crucial for preventing thrombotic events while minimizing bleeding risk. This guide covers weight-based heparin protocols and PTT-based adjustments.

## Understanding Heparin Basics

### Units and Concentrations

Standard heparin concentrations:
- **Standard concentration**: 1,000 units/mL
- **Premixed bags**: 25,000 units in 250 mL (100 units/mL)
- **High concentration**: 5,000 units/mL or 10,000 units/mL

### Weight-Based Protocols

Most protocols use actual body weight with some using adjusted body weight for obesity.

## Initial Dosing Calculations

### Step 1: Calculate Bolus Dose

```
Bolus dose = Weight (kg) × Bolus dose per kg
```

**Standard bolus**: 80 units/kg

**Example**:
- Patient weight: 75 kg
- Bolus = 75 kg × 80 units/kg = 6,000 units

### Step 2: Calculate Initial Infusion Rate

```
Initial rate (units/hr) = Weight (kg) × Initial dose per kg/hr
```

**Standard initial rate**: 18 units/kg/hr

**Example**:
- Patient weight: 75 kg
- Rate = 75 kg × 18 units/kg/hr = 1,350 units/hr

### Step 3: Convert to mL/hr

```
mL/hr = Units/hr ÷ Concentration (units/mL)
```

**Example**:
- Rate needed: 1,350 units/hr
- Concentration: 100 units/mL
- mL/hr = 1,350 ÷ 100 = 13.5 mL/hr

## PTT-Based Adjustments

### Standard Adjustment Protocol

| PTT (seconds) | Bolus | Rate Change | Recheck PTT |
|--------------|-------|-------------|-------------|
| < 35 | 80 units/kg | ↑ 4 units/kg/hr | 6 hours |
| 35-45 | 40 units/kg | ↑ 2 units/kg/hr | 6 hours |
| 46-70 | None | No change | Next AM |
| 71-90 | None | ↓ 2 units/kg/hr | 6 hours |
| > 90 | Hold 1 hr | ↓ 3 units/kg/hr | 6 hours |

### Calculating Adjustments

**Example**: PTT returns at 40 seconds
- Current rate: 1,350 units/hr (18 units/kg/hr)
- Action: Give 40 units/kg bolus, increase by 2 units/kg/hr
- Bolus: 75 kg × 40 = 3,000 units
- New rate: 20 units/kg/hr = 1,500 units/hr = 15 mL/hr

## Special Considerations

### Obesity Adjustments

For patients > 120% ideal body weight:
```
Adjusted weight = IBW + 0.4(Actual weight - IBW)
```

### Maximum Dose Limits

Many protocols have maximum limits:
- **Max bolus**: 10,000 units
- **Max initial rate**: 2,000 units/hr

## Practice Problems

### Problem 1: Initial Dosing
Patient: 82 kg
Calculate bolus and initial infusion rate.

**Solution**:
- Bolus: 82 × 80 = 6,560 units
- Initial rate: 82 × 18 = 1,476 units/hr
- mL/hr: 1,476 ÷ 100 = 14.8 mL/hr

### Problem 2: PTT Adjustment
Current: 1,200 units/hr (16 units/kg/hr) on 75 kg patient
PTT: 38 seconds

**Solution**:
- Give bolus: 75 × 40 = 3,000 units
- Increase rate by 2 units/kg/hr
- New rate: 18 units/kg/hr = 1,350 units/hr = 13.5 mL/hr

## Documentation Requirements

Always document:
- Patient weight used
- Bolus dose given
- Current infusion rate
- PTT results and time
- Any rate adjustments
- Next PTT due time

## Safety Tips

1. **Always use a pump** - Never gravity drip heparin
2. **Label clearly** - Mark concentration prominently
3. **Independent double-check** - Have another nurse verify
4. **Monitor for bleeding** - Check all sites
5. **Have protamine available** - Know reversal protocol

Remember: Heparin protocols are high-risk. Always follow your institution's specific protocol and have calculations verified by another healthcare provider.
